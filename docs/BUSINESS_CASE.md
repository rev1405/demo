# MallHaul Business Case

## Problem
South African shoppers visit 3-5 stores per mall trip. Online, each store is
a separate checkout, separate delivery fee, separate tracking. Malls watch
single-retailer apps skim their footfall without a mall-level answer.

## Solution
MallHaul is the mall's own digital layer: every store in the mall becomes
shippable in one trolley, one payment, one consolidated delivery through the
mall's fulfillment hub, with Kasi runners as the last mile.

## Revenue model
1. Platform fee: 3 percent of basket (visible in every payment split).
2. Delivery: R35 base (hub consolidated) / R55 door-to-door.
3. Mall SaaS (phase 2): ops dashboard + analytics subscription per mall.
4. Sponsored placement (phase 3): store ranking inside a mall.

## Market and wedge
- Beachhead: one flagship mall (Gateway, Durban) then 5 malls in KZN year 1.
- Why now: mall hubs already exist for click-and-collect; we reuse them.
- Defensibility: per-mall exclusivity + consolidated delivery economics no
  single retailer can replicate.

## Unit economics (seed data reflects this)
Average basket R320 -> platform fee R9.60 + delivery margin on the R35/R55
split across stores; courier paid per completed run. Break-even per mall at
roughly 30 orders/day (hub staffed by the mall - asset-light).

## Ethics stance
- Couriers: per-run pay with distance-minimising assignment - their unpaid
  travel decreases as our cost decreases (incentives aligned).
- Stores: no exclusionary contracts.
- Customers: POPIA-aligned minimal data; full audit trail for disputes.
